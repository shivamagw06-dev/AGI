Portfolio-level decisions before adding weights

  1. Keep the copied reference portfolio and an expanded AGI variant separate. Preserve the original capture date and record every rebalance.
  2. Obtain current prices and diluted share counts, reconcile trailing and forecast earnings, and build bear/base/bull values. Strong businesses can be poor investments at the wrong price.
  3. For loss-making companies use cash runway, dilution and milestones; for REITs use AFFO and leverage; for miners use mid-cycle commodity prices; for insurers use underwriting and reserves. One PE screen cannot cover all candidates.
  4. Measure look-through overlap across direct stocks and ETFs. ANET, ORCL, PWR and GEV recur here. Different themes can still depend on the same AI capital-spending cycle.
  5. Test proposed allocations against the original basket and an appropriate benchmark, including dividends, fees, currency effects and rebalancing. No improvement in expected return or drawdown has been established by this research.
  6. Confirm the exact security and broker availability. USA-listed ADRs may represent foreign businesses; ETFs and single stocks have different tax and distribution treatment. No broker execution is connected to this shortlist.
