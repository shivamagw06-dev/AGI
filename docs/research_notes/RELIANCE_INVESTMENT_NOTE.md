## 1. Executive summary

Reliance Industries is India’s largest listed conglomerate by market relevance, spanning **Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C)**, **Retail**, **Digital (Jio)**, and a growing **New Energy / materials** agenda. The institutional question is not whether the franchise exists — it does — but whether **today’s price already discounts** the next phase of cash conversion, capital intensity, and conglomerate complexity.

**Investment view (edited):** Remain **Neutral / Monitor**. Own only with explicit segment NAV discipline and a checklist for O2C cycle, retail execution, Jio ARPU/capex, and new-energy cash burn. Do **not** treat the raw AGIB SELL label as actionable — it was produced on incomplete inputs with **incorrect sector monitors** (see corrections log).
