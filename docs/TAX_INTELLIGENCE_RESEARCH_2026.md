# AGI Tax Intelligence: research and decision map

Research date: 25 September 2026. The client scanner is a review aid, not a filed return or universal tax opinion. Rules here are year-specific; links to the Income Tax Department, CBDT and CBIC are recorded below. The 2025 Act uses Tax Year 2026–27 beginning 1 April 2026. FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27 continues under the 1961 Act. Never reuse the earlier numerical rule package for the current tax year.

## What a good client result requires
For each taxpayer: year and residence; source and legal ownership of each receipt; income head; actual transaction date; evidence; regime election history; eligible deductions; TDS/advance-tax credits; outstanding conflicts; prospective net cash cost, lock-in and risk. Compare *whole-case* tax after allowed deductions and rate differences, then identify whether the client can still take an action in the applicable tax year. An unused deduction ceiling is not itself tax saved. A tax-saving product can cost money, lock up capital or have unsuitable returns.

| Rented property | For qualifying house-property income, gross annual value, municipal taxes actually paid by owner, 30% of net annual value and eligible interest can matter. | Ownership shares, leases, rent, municipal receipts and debt use; higher reasonable rent/vacancy and losses need detailed handling. Composite letting and hotel services can change income head. | Review queue; no simplistic tax estimate. |
| Property / investment sale | Cost basis, FIFO/corporate actions, timing and prescribed sections 54/54F/54EC may affect gain. | Deeds, inherited previous-owner cost, dates, stamp value, eligible asset, reinvestment window, CGAS and lock-in. | Opportunity to examine, never assert exemption just for buying property. |
| Business / hotel | Review documented business-only expenses, depreciation, permitted presumptive method vs actual books. | Business purpose, invoices, payroll, asset use and turnover. Presumptive income is not followed by another deduction for the same expenses. | Review, no auto-deducting personal purchases. |
| GST | Separate supply tax from income tax; classify hotel rooms and each lease, supplier/recipient registration and ITC. | GSTIN, invoices, room rates/supply dates, contract and ITC ledger. Rules and rates can change. | Routing checklist only until a GST rule package is verified. |

| AIS/26AS | Catch omitted income, duplicated imported lines and missing tax credits. | AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, bank and broker originals. TDS is a credit; a mismatched record needs investigation, not automatic duplication. | Always first action. |

## Further qualifying cases, not numerically implemented
80E education-loan interest, 80G donations, HRA/80GG, 80DD/80U, employer NPS, self-occupied property interest, house-property loss set-off, senior advance-tax relief, capital-loss harvesting, foreign tax credits and business presumptive eligibility require separate facts and tests. A donor receipt is not enough for a 100% 80G estimate; eligible donee, donation mode and qualifying limits vary. No generic 'move deposits to mother', 'buy land to cancel FD tax', 'claim GST as income tax', or 'pay a business expense to save 100% tax' recommendation is valid.

## Implementation / validation boundary
The new scanner performs no official portal imports, OCR, persistent record storage, tax filing or individualized professional certification. It reuses the existing FY25–26 ordinary-income estimator only for a complete individual salary/interest fact pattern. It does not treat current-year law as an extension of the previous assessment year. Quantitative comparisons are estimates pending independent professional rule verification and real case reconciliation. Rules for other case types remain work to build, not silent zeros.

### Evidence workspace extension
The September 25 extension supports client-selected CSV column mapping (income, credit, category, reference, date), a row review queue, discrepancy and possible-duplicate checks, year/owner assignment, and import of *user-provided* Upstox mutual fund order JSON. Orders remain investment evidence: pending/failed orders are not transactions and completed redemptions cannot establish capital gains without independently matched cost and units. A single qualifying let-out property can be calculated as a provisional annual-value schedule; this is never silently added to the narrow whole-return regime comparison. For FY25–26 the simulator compares the benefit of 80C/NPS contributions **already paid in that year** against the best of the supported regimes, with no new-year investment suggestion.

The review can be downloaded as JSON and reopened. Reopening requires each row and both income and deduction confirmations to be checked again. The browser keeps source records in memory only; AGI has no tax-document database or per-client broker OAuth in this release. CSV mapping is deliberately explicit because official AIS and Form 168 exports do not have a validated field mapping in AGI. PDF, ITR-pre-filled JSON, Form 16 and Form 26AS are **not** automatically parsed. CSV imports are capped at 2 MB and 1,000 rows, and duplicate flags block numerical comparison until resolved. Review exports contain personal financial information and should be stored by the client securely.

### Personalized decisions
The decision brief ranks unresolved evidence first, then a whole-case regime comparison where the narrow FY25–26 estimate is available. It asks targeted follow-up questions for HRA, employer NPS, health cover, donations, property, inherited sales and mutual fund orders, business/hotel receipts, GST, foreign income and interest tax credits. These cases remain unquantified unless the specific deduction and all relevant income are supported. Confirmed 80D premiums are capped separately for self/family and parents and included only in the supported FY25–26 estimate; the decision card measures any incremental effect against the lower regime, which may be zero. Reopening a saved review restores premium amounts but resets all eligibility confirmations. In TY26–27 the interface collects facts and points to Schedule XV / section 123 without displaying a guessed tax saving.

Tax Year 2026–27 uses Form No. 168 as the successor to AIS under the 2025 Act. The AY2026–27 AIS format and year-specific rule package must not be reused for that tax year. Official transition: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/tax-payments

### Further release gates
1. Validate actual redacted AIS / Form 168, Form 26AS and income-tax prefill fixtures for their individual years. Add independent category/subcategory mapping and official-source reconciliation tests. Do not assume all CSV columns carry taxable income or that Form 168 is available to every client today.
2. Independently reproduce TY2026–27 income heads, old/new regime rules, surcharge, rebate, deductions, losses, credits and advance-tax schedules against the Income Tax Department calculator before displaying a current-year tax number.
3. Add transaction-lot and corporate-action reconciliation for CAS, brokers and funds; direct user-specific Upstox access needs each client's authorized OAuth and an appropriately secured broker connection. The shared AGI market-data bearer token must never be used to read a client's orders.
4. Build property-by-property books, hotel income classification, GST invoice/ITC reconciliation, and multi-owner schedules from reviewed contracts/invoices and separately maintained, dated legal rules.
5. Only after evidence provenance, access control, retention/deletion, consent, and regulatory review are complete, assess whether an ERI integration for return preparation/submission is appropriate. Official API overview: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/api-specifications

The Income Tax Department confirms AIS downloads in PDF, JSON and CSV and states AIS may omit relevant transactions; TIS also contains category-level processed and confirmed values. A reliable importer must be tested against real redacted, year-specific export fixtures before it can map columns or deduplicate AIS/TIS/26AS. The current scanner takes user-entered facts and does not import these files. Official reference: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/ais-faq
