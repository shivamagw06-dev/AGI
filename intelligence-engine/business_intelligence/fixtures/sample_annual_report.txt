AMPLE INDUSTRIALS LIMITED
Annual Report FY2026

Business Overview
Ample Industrials is a diversified manufacturing company. The operating model centres on
integrated production campuses with captive power. The revenue model includes product sales
and recurring maintenance contracts.

Products and Services
Products include specialty chemicals, industrial adhesives, and packaging films.
The company provides engineering services and aftermarket support.

Geographic Exposure
Geographic markets include India, North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific.

Customer Profile
Customers include industrial OEMs with moderate client concentration in the top ten accounts.
Distribution channels: direct enterprise sales and authorised distributors.
Supply chain references cover critical feedstock sourcing and logistics partners.

Management Discussion and Analysis
Management expects strong growth driven by exports and capacity expansion.
Operating leverage benefits are referenced in the MD&A discussion of fixed-cost absorption.

Business Segments
Operating segments include Specialty Chemicals, Adhesives, Packaging Films, and Engineering Services.
Revenue by segment is disclosed with year-over-year changes versus previous year.
Profit by segment and capital allocation by segment are summarised in notes.

Strategy
Growth strategy emphasises expand export business and industry expansion.
Expansion plans include greenfield capacity in India.
Cost optimisation and efficiency programmes continue alongside digital initiatives in cloud and generative AI capabilities.
Capacity expansion is underway at two campuses.
Product launches in high-performance adhesives are planned.
Acquisitions remain selective; no material divestitures were completed in the period.
Capital expenditure supports capacity and digital programmes. Debt reduction remains a priority.

Capital Allocation
Dividend policy and shareholder returns are described.
Buybacks may be considered subject to board approval.
Liquidity remains adequate for near-term obligations.
Cash deployment prioritises organic growth and debt repayment.
Investment priorities include capacity, R&D, and digital productivity tools.

Risk Factors
Key risks include commodity price volatility, currency movement, interest rates, competition,
regulation, technology disruption, project execution risk, supply chain disruption,
customer concentration, climate-related physical risks, and cyber security threats.

Guidance
Revenue guidance for FY2027 is disclosed as mid-to-high single digit growth.
Margin guidance comments on gradual margin improvement initiatives.
Capex guidance outlines elevated investment for two years.
Demand outlook remains constructive in export markets.
Industry outlook notes orderly competitive intensity.
Cost outlook highlights wage and feedstock vigilance.

Corporate Governance Report
Board composition and committee structures are disclosed.
Related-party transaction oversight is described.
Auditor changes: none during the year.
Management changes were limited to one business-unit leadership appointment.
