# Institutional Decision Readiness Engine (IDRE) V1

**Programme:** RQ2 — Institutional Reasoning  
**Sprint:** 9  
**Architecture:** v1.0.1 LOCKED — final soft-wired quality gate, not a top-level layer

## Position

```
Institutional Debate Engine
        ↓
IDRE (Decision Readiness)
        ↓
Investment Committee
```

## Primary question

> Is this investment thesis decision-ready, and what remains unresolved?

## Readiness dimensions

Evidence 30% · Reasoning 20% · Debate 15% · Portfolio 15% · Monitoring 10% · Policy 10%

Confidence is reported separately as a seventh heat-map dimension.

## States

`READY` · `READY WITH CONDITIONS` · `RESEARCH REQUIRED` · `NOT READY`

## Extensions

- **Decision Heat Map** — makes the weak dimension immediately visible
- **Objective Go / No-Go Conditions** — current, threshold, distance and automatic review action
- **Capital Allocation Readiness** — explicitly separate from thesis quality and decision readiness
