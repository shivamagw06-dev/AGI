# AGI CRE Investment Research Benchmark

## Baseline

- Canonical industry models: **36** (not 38)
- Permanent CRE questions: **144** (four per industry)
- End-to-end industry readiness threshold: **8/10**
- Baseline passing industries: **4/36 (11.11%)**
- Mean readiness score: **7.33/10**

## What the baseline means

All 36 Industry DNA records contain core economics, KPIs, valuation methods,
risks, and KPI relationships. Only banks, FMCG, IT services, and metals also
have both existing causal-graph coverage and a linked gold-standard company.
The remaining 32 models are therefore knowledge-complete at the schema level
but not yet end-to-end causal/company ready.

## Highest-priority gaps

1. Add governed CIG coverage for the 32 uncovered industries.
2. Link at least one evidence-rich NSE gold company to each industry.
3. Run the four benchmark questions for every industry at a fixed information
   cutoff and preserve the answer plus scorecard.
4. Obtain independent analyst gold answers before claiming economic accuracy.

The benchmark does not claim that populated Industry DNA is economically
correct. It measures structural readiness. Economic correctness still requires
independent analyst comparison on identical evidence and point-in-time cutoffs.
