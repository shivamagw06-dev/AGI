# Institutional Research Writer (IRW V1)

Presentation + writing layer only. **Not an engine.**

Sits **after** the Chief Investment Officer.

## Locked

Does not redesign CID, Company Analysis, Financial Intelligence, Investment Committee,
CIO, DVC, providers, Knowledge Foundation, Academy, or Institutional Analysts.

Never changes recommendations, scores, confidence, votes, or evidence.

## Flow

```text
Analysts → Committee → CIO → Institutional Research Writer → Final Report
```
